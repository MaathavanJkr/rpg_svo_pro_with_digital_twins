# Related work (literature check, 2026-10-26)

Every entry below was located online during this check (URL given). Venue details were confirmed from the paper itself or from an index page where noted; anything not confirmed is marked. Summaries describe what the cited source states; claims about what a paper does not contain are limited to what was read (abstract, or full text where stated).

## (a) Distance-field / SDF registration for map-based localisation and drift correction

| Work | What it does | Relation to this project |
|---|---|---|
| **Merat, Cioffi, Bauersfeld, Scaramuzza, "Drift-free Visual SLAM using Digital Twins", IEEE RA-L 10(2):1633–1640, 2025** ([arXiv 2412.08496](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.08496)) | Registers the sparse VIO landmark cloud to a city-scale digital twin with point-to-plane ICP and fuses the result as a global pose in SVO Pro's backend; GPS-simulator and real-drone experiments (urban). | The base system (M2). We replace only the registration step and evaluate indoors. |
| **Huang, Sun, Ye, Liu, "Metric Monocular Localization Using Signed Distance Fields", IROS 2019** ([arXiv 2003.14157](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.14157); full text read) | Monocular (ORB-SLAM2 front end, no IMU) localisation against an SDF prior map (Voxblox, 0.1 m voxels). Distance factor φ(Tp) with trilinear gradient, Huber loss, LM, χ²-based outlier rejection (th_sdf = 3.841), tightly coupled with reprojection factors; joint optimisation of structure and poses. EuRoC (MH01/V101/V201, map built from dense stereo reconstruction **using ground-truth poses**) and KITTI; initialisation-robustness test up to 0.5 m / 16°. | **Point-to-distance-field registration against a prior SDF on EuRoC, including χ² gating and an initialisation test, is published.** Differences: their map is built with GT poses (not independent); monocular without IMU; no comparison with point-to-plane in the same system. |
| **Huang, Ye, Sun, Liu, "GMMLoc: Structure Consistent Visual Localization with Gaussian Mixture Models", IEEE RA-L 2020** (DOI 10.1109/LRA.2020.3005130; [arXiv 2006.13670](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.13670); full text read) | Stereo (no IMU) localisation against a GMM prior map of the EuRoC Vicon rooms built from the dataset's dense reconstruction; all six V1/V2 sequences; ATE 1.8–5.6 cm. | Prior-map localisation on the same rooms with better accuracy than our map-frame errors (6.6–10.6 cm, unaligned; their alignment protocol not checked). Our work is not a new state of the art in accuracy. |
| **Zuo, Geneva, Yang, Ye, Liu, Huang, "Visual-Inertial Localization With Prior LiDAR Map Constraints", IEEE RA-L 4(4), 2019** (DOI 10.1109/LRA.2019.2927123; [PDF](https://pgeneva.com/downloads/preprints/Zuo2019RAL.pdf)) | Stereo MSCKF VIO; semi-dense visual clouds registered to a prior LiDAR map with NDT; registrations fused as global measurements in a tightly coupled update. | Closest architecture to M2/M3 (VIO cloud → map registration → global measurement), with NDT instead of point-to-plane or a distance field. |
| **Caselitz, Steder, Ruhnke, Burgard, "Monocular camera localization in 3D LiDAR maps", IROS 2016, pp. 1926–1931** (confirmed via the reference list of Huang et al. 2019; publisher page not retrieved) | Matches a VO/BA point cloud to a LiDAR map to track the camera. | Early instance of the cloud-to-map approach. |
| **Caballero, Merino, "DLL: Direct LIDAR Localization. A map-based localization approach for aerial robots", IROS 2021, pp. 5491–5498** ([arXiv 2103.06112](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.06112); [code](https://github.com/h3ct0r/dll)) | Registers LiDAR scans to a prebuilt map by non-linear optimisation of point-to-map distances without correspondences, an order of magnitude faster than comparable optimisation-based methods (abstract). | The distance-to-map registration principle for aerial robots, with LiDAR instead of sparse VIO landmarks. |
| Classic SDF alignment: Fitzgibbon (BMVC 2001/IVC 2003, LM-ICP with distance transform); Newcombe et al., KinectFusion (ISMAR 2011); Bylow et al. (RSS 2013); Oleynikova et al., Voxblox (IROS 2017); Reijgwart et al., Voxgraph ([arXiv 2004.13154](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.13154), RA-L 2020) | Point-to-SDF alignment and SDF maps for MAVs. | Method foundations (already cited in the project plan; bibliographic details to be re-verified). |
| Pan et al., "Multi-Resolution Voxelized Map-Based Stereo Visual-Inertial Odometry", IROS 2026 ([arXiv 2607.17332](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17332); abstract read) | Voxelised prior map (one point per voxel) with 2D–3D feature association for stereo VIO. | Recent map-based VIO; voxel map but not a distance field (abstract). |

**What our work adds (honest):** distance-field registration against a prior map is not new, and neither is χ²-gated SDF localisation on EuRoC (Huang et al. 2019). What does not appear in the sources found is (i) a controlled, pre-registered comparison of point-to-plane vs distance-field registration **inside the same tightly coupled VIO + digital-twin system** (only the registration step changed, identical inputs offline and online), (ii) with an **independent** laser-scan twin (not built from GT poses or from the same sensor data), and (iii) the resulting systems findings: the throughput effect of the original implementation, the weighting/outlier interaction, and the scale dependence of the original re-alignment criterion.

## (b) Information weighting, consistency and gating of map-registration / global-pose measurements

| Work | Content | Relation |
|---|---|---|
| **Censi, "An Accurate Closed-form Estimate of ICP's Covariance", ICRA 2007, pp. 3167–3172** (confirmed via the reference list of Brossard et al.) | Hessian-based closed-form ICP covariance. | The "Hessian" information arm of D4 is of this family. |
| **Brossard, Bonnabel, Barrau, "A New Approach to 3D ICP Covariance Estimation", IEEE RA-L 2020** (DOI 10.1109/LRA.2020.2965391; [arXiv 1909.05722](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.05722)) | Shows that ICP covariance must also account for initialisation uncertainty, wrong convergence and under-constrained geometry (unscented transform), beyond Censi's closed form. | Consistent with our finding that the raw Hessian is overconfident (×2.4–4.5 in position) and that online closed-loop errors exceed open-loop ones. |
| **Zhang et al., "Map-based Visual-Inertial Localization: Consistency and Complexity", IEEE RA-L 2023** ([arXiv 2204.12173](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.12173)) | Consistency of map-based VIL with map uncertainty (Schmidt EKF, keyframe maps). | Consistency of map-based VIO is studied; our P1 concerns gating/weighting of registration measurements in an optimisation backend. |
| **Sünderhauf, Protzel, "Switchable constraints for robust pose graph SLAM", IROS 2012** ([QUT eprints](https://eprints.qut.edu.au/109582)); **Agarwal et al., "Robust map optimization using dynamic covariance scaling", ICRA 2013** ([PDF](https://www.ipb.uni-bonn.de/wp-content/papercite-data/pdf/agarwal13icra.pdf)) | Robust back-ends that deactivate or down-weight outlier constraints. | Candidate mechanisms for the "robust gating" future work (P1). |
| χ² gating in VIO and fusion filters: Huang et al. 2019 (above, SDF factor); collaborative MAV localisation with a 6-DoF χ² test on pose measurements ([arXiv 1905.02648](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.02648)); dimension-specific χ² gates for 6-DoF VSLAM poses in a fusion filter ([arXiv 2605.25239](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.25239), preprint) | Mahalanobis/χ² gates on pose or map measurements are standard practice. | **χ² gating of global-pose measurements is not novel by itself.** What P1 would add is an evaluation of calibrated gating for digital-twin registration in a closed loop, building on our D4 evidence that strong weights without gating fail. |

## (d) Public datasets with visual-inertial data and an independent prior map

| **Hilti-Oxford 2022** (Zhang et al., IEEE RA-L 8(1):408–415, 2023, DOI 10.1109/LRA.2022.3226077; [challenge page](https://hilti-challenge.com/dataset-2022.html)) | Full terrestrial laser scans (Z+F Imager 5016) of the sites released as .e57 | Alphasense Core, 5 global-shutter cameras + IMU (+ Hesai lidar) | millimetre-accurate **sparse** positions from surveyed reference points (denser 6-DoF for some sequences) | GT from survey points, map from TLS: **largely independent** (both survey-based; check the registration of scan and control points) | **CC BY-NC-SA 3.0** (stated on the page): academic use allowed, non-commercial, share-alike |
| **Newer College** ("The Newer College Dataset: Handheld LiDAR, Inertial and Vision with Ground Truth", [arXiv 2003.05691](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.05691); authors/venue not verified in this check; [page](https://ori-drs.github.io/newer-college-dataset/)) | Leica BLK360 survey map (~290 M points); download availability not stated on the page | RealSense D435i or Alphasense multi-camera + IMU | **inferred from the survey map** (lidar scans registered to the map) | **not independent**: GT is derived from the same map, which would favour any map-based method | not stated on the page (to be confirmed with the authors) |

**Implication for P4:** Hilti-Oxford 2022 is the most suitable second dataset (independent survey ground truth, released scans, cameras + IMU, academic licence), with the caveat that its GT is sparse (evaluation at control points only). Newer College is unsuitable for map-frame accuracy claims because its GT is derived from the map.

## Summary: what is and is not new
- **Already published:** point-to-SDF registration for map-based visual localisation (Huang et al. 2019), including χ² gating and an initialisation-robustness test on EuRoC; cloud-to-map registration fused as global measurements in VIO (Zuo et al. 2019; Merat et al. 2025); distance-field registration for aerial LiDAR localisation (DLL); prior-map localisation on the EuRoC Vicon rooms with cm-level accuracy (GMMLoc); ICP covariance overconfidence (Censi; Brossard et al.).
- **Not found in the sources checked (candidate contributions):** a controlled, pre-registered comparison of point-to-plane vs distance-field registration within one tightly coupled VIO + digital-twin system with an independent twin; the closed-loop finding that calibrated (Hessian) weighting without gating degrades a twin-registration system while the heuristic's underconfidence protects it; the room-vs-city scale dependence of the original system's components (KD-tree throughput, re-alignment criterion); robustness to tampered digital twins (to be re-checked).
