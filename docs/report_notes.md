# Report notes (running collection for the write-up)

## Stated assumptions
- **A1. One-time initial alignment from GT positions over the first 2 m of flight (EuRoC).** The VIO frame is aligned to the digital-twin frame once, by a 4-DoF (yaw + translation) least-squares fit of the VIO positions to the ground-truth positions from the first keyframe with an initialised backend until the ground-truth path length reaches 2 m (~8 s on V1_01; same 2 m as the original pipeline's GPS alignment distance). Roll and pitch come from the VIO gravity estimate; ground-truth orientation is not used. This stands in for a known take-off pose and heading. Ground truth is not used online after the window, and map-frame errors are evaluated only after it. The same procedure applies to all methods (M1 offline, M2/M3 online, numerically identical), and the transform is held fixed in the main experiments (`refine_T_WL: false`). *Justification:* EuRoC's ground-truth orientation on V1 is inconsistent with the raw Vicon orientation by a few degrees; an orientation-based heading was ~7° off the position-based one, and the twin geometry independently indicated a ~4° heading error. Part of that offset may come from early VIO heading error rather than ground truth. *(Decisions D1, D1(B), 2026-10-23; docs/frames.md.)*
- **A2. Map frame = Vicon frame (EuRoC).** The Leica scan is given in the dataset's world frame (`pointcloud0/sensor.yaml`, `T_WR = I`), so map-frame errors are computed directly against the Vicon ground truth without any trajectory alignment. The scan-to-Vicon registration accuracy of the dataset itself is an error floor we do not control.
- **A3. Identical map input.** M2 and M3 load the same preprocessed scan file (cropped, downsampled, with normals), produced once offline. *(Decision D3.)*
- **A4. Identical measurement weighting (main comparison).** M3 reports its information matrix in M2's heuristic convention (normalised ∇D in place of the surface normal). A Hessian-based covariance for both is an ablation (2×2). *(Decision D4.)*

## Facts worth citing
- D4 weighting ablation (Phase 10, 180 runs): calibrated Hessian significantly WORSE than the heuristic for both methods (family A, 8/8 tests, +85 to +156 mm, Holm p ≤ 2e-4); raw Hessian diverges; M3 vs M2F under calibrated weights not different (family B). Heuristic M3 vs M2F on V2_02: −12.6 mm (secondary, p 0.006), third batch in that direction. Exploratory: wrong-but-accepted registrations compound under strong weights (docs/phase10_d4_ablation.md).
- H3 throughput control (Phase 10, report wording): "Throughput control inconclusive under the pre-registered rule: with throughput equalised, point-to-plane's mean was unchanged (0.094 vs 0.095 m), and M3 remained 20 mm lower on V2_02 (unadjusted 95% CI [−38, −2] mm; Holm p = 0.073)." (docs/phase10_throughput.md)
- H3 (Phase 9, report wording): "M3 matched M2 within ±1.4 cm (95 % CI) on V1_01 and V1_02, and was 14 mm lower on V2_02 (Holm p = 0.016); attribution to the algorithm pending the throughput control." On V1_02 M2 dropped 28 % of clouds yet matched M3. M1 vs digital twin (pre-registered secondary): p ≤ 6e-5 on V1_02 and V2_02 (survives any correction); V1_01 local-consistency cost in the discussion. V2_03: limitation with V1_03, descriptive only (docs/phase9_online.md).
- M3 solver (Phase 8): "Levenberg–Marquardt on SE(3) in C++/Eigen, verified against the prototype to ~1e-13" (docs/phase8_cpp.md).
- H2 headline: ~10× per iteration (P2P figure indirect) and ~4–5× per solve without M2's per-call KD-tree rebuild; 135× only as a labelled property of M2's implementation (docs/phase8_cpp.md §7).
- The original pipeline only activates map registration after a GPS-based 4-DoF alignment. Registered poses enter the Ceres window as absolute pose priors (Huber δ = 50) on the keyframe with a matching stamp (±1 ms). *(docs/pipeline_notes.md)*

## Limitations (draft)
- **Validity checks do not reliably detect wrong registrations.** In the worst throughput-control run (V2_02 M2F r05, docs/phase10_throughput.md §5) registrations whose poses were ~0.6 m from GT were accepted with fitness ≈ 1: fitness (fraction of points within the 0.5 m correspondence radius) and inlier RMSE stay normal when the cloud locks onto the wrong but nearby surfaces. This is the room-scale counterpart of the Phase 3 note (5a) that fitness is uninformative when the correspondence radius is large relative to the scene. Applies to M2 and M3 (same checks).
- V1_03 and V2_03: SVO full resets (limitation, descriptive only; docs/phase9_online.md).
- Acceptance limits (5°, 0.75 m) were derived on V1 and not retuned for V2 (M2 effectively VIO-only on V2_03).
- **Backend robust loss tuned for weak weights.** The registered-pose residual in the Ceres backend uses HuberLoss(50) (svo_ceres_backend, unchanged from the original). It was set for the heuristic information, whose weights are weak (position σ ≈ 0.3–0.4 m, yaw σ ≈ 60°, docs/phase10_d4_realism.md); with the (calibrated) Hessian the whitened residuals are much larger, so the same threshold down-weights far fewer outliers. Not changed in the D4 ablation (by design).
- The original heuristic information is expressed about the map origin while the backend reads it as body-pose information; kept unchanged in M2/M3 (faithful baseline); the Hessian arms of D4 use the body-centred form.
